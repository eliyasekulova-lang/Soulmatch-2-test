# Critical Safety Playbook (Attorney Review Required)

## Scope
- CSAM indicators
- Minor sexual exploitation indicators
- Violent threat indicators

## Required Handling
1. Immediate quarantine of reported content and linked media metadata.
2. Force report status to `in_review` and escalation status to `critical_queue`.
3. Create immutable moderation action and audit record.
4. Assign moderation + legal owner and open an incident record.
5. Preserve evidence references and chain-of-custody notes.
6. Process lawful disclosure only through the law-enforcement request workflow.

## SLA Targets
- Triage start: under 15 minutes.
- Decision checkpoint: under 60 minutes.
- Final action/log closure: under 24 hours.

## Legal Hold
- Do not hard-delete evidence under active legal hold.
- Retention exceptions must be approved by legal and logged.

Final legal standards and regional obligations require counsel sign-off before public launch.
