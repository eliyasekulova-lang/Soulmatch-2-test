# Sensitive Data Consent Notice (v1.0)

Status: Draft for attorney review before public launch.
Effective date: 2026-02-14

## Consent Statement
By accepting, you explicitly consent to processing your birth date, birth time, and birth place as sensitive profile inputs for compatibility and personalization services.

## Purpose Limitation
Sensitive data is used for:
- astrology chart computation,
- compatibility ranking,
- explanation features,
- safety/compliance obligations where required by law.

## Withdrawal
You can withdraw consent in-app. Withdrawal is recorded as an immutable consent event and may limit matching functionality.

## Evidence Capture
Consent events store:
- policy version,
- timestamp,
- locale/jurisdiction/platform,
- IP/user-agent/app version,
- immutable consent hash.
