# Minors and Age-Gating Policy (v1.0)

Status: Draft for attorney review before public launch.
Effective date: 2026-02-14

## Minimum Age
- Account creation requires a date of birth at signup.
- Signup is blocked if user age is below the configured minimum age threshold.

## High-Risk Minor Safety Signals
If reports include minor-risk reason codes (`minor`, `csam`, `sexual_content_minor`), the platform must:
1. move report to critical review queue,
2. preserve evidence metadata,
3. restrict exposure while investigation is ongoing,
4. record moderation and audit entries.

## Enforcement
Violation of child-safety rules results in immediate escalated moderation and potential account suspension/ban.

## Legal Review Notice
This policy requires external legal counsel approval prior to public launch.
