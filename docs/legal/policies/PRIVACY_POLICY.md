# SoulMatch Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026  
**Applies To:** SoulMatch web and mobile applications and related services

SoulMatch ("we," "us," "our") is an astrology-based and AI-powered matching platform. We are committed to protecting personal information in line with Canada’s **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** and international privacy best practices.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account information:** email address, phone number, login credentials, account settings.
- **Profile information:** name, birth date, birth time, birth city/country (and derived location/timezone metadata for chart calculations).
- **Psychology onboarding information:** responses to non-diagnostic onboarding questions.
- **Behavioral interaction signals:** likes, passes, matches, message activity metadata, response timing patterns, and engagement signals.
- **Device and usage information:** IP address, device identifiers, app version, browser type, operating system, logs, and usage analytics.
- **Safety and abuse prevention signals:** reports, blocks, suspicious activity indicators, spam/fraud patterns, and policy enforcement events.

## 2. How We Use Information
- matching and compatibility analysis,
- improving match quality and personalization,
- generating profile insights and compatibility explanations,
- safety, fraud, abuse detection and prevention,
- system optimization and service improvement,
- legal and compliance obligations.

## 3. AI and Automated Processing Disclosure
SoulMatch uses AI and automated systems to analyze profile, onboarding, and behavioral data to improve recommendations and safety. SoulMatch does **not** use fully automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant legal effects without appropriate human oversight.

## 4. Trust & Safety Processing
Behavioral and interaction signals may be processed to detect spam, fraud, harassment, coercion-risk patterns, and policy violations. Safety actions may include reduced visibility, verification requirements, and messaging limitations. SoulMatch does **not** label users with clinical or psychological diagnoses.

## 5. Data Sharing
We may share data with:
- service providers supporting hosting, security, analytics, communications, and operations,
- legal/regulatory authorities where required,
- professional advisors for compliance obligations.

We do **not sell personal data**.

## 6. Data Retention
Data is retained only as necessary for identified purposes, legal obligations, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and safety enforcement. Approved deletion requests are removed from active systems and backup copies are purged on scheduled retention windows.

## 7. User Rights
Subject to applicable law, users may request:
- access,
- correction,
- deletion,
- withdrawal of consent (where processing is consent-based).

## 8. Security Measures
SoulMatch applies safeguards including encryption in transit/at rest where appropriate, access controls, monitoring/logging, and incident response procedures.
