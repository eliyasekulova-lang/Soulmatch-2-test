# SoulMatch AI and Automated Processing Disclosure
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026

SoulMatch uses automated systems to:
- generate match recommendations,
- analyze behavioral patterns,
- detect spam, fraud, and harassment risk,
- improve platform safety.

AI assists operational decision-making, but does not make legally binding decisions with legal or similarly significant effects without safeguards.

SoulMatch does not provide psychological or medical diagnoses, and does not label users with clinical psychological classifications.

In line with Canadian privacy law (including PIPEDA principles), SoulMatch provides transparency about processing purposes, applies safeguards proportionate to data sensitivity, and supports user rights requests.
