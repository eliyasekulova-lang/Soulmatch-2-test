# SoulMatch Acceptable Use Policy
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026

## Prohibited Behaviors
Users must not engage in:
- harassment,
- coercion,
- manipulation,
- impersonation,
- spam,
- fraud,
- abuse,
- exploiting vulnerable users,
- automated scraping/data extraction,
- bypassing safety restrictions.

## Enforcement Actions
SoulMatch may apply one or more actions:
- feature restrictions,
- verification requirements,
- reduced visibility,
- account suspension,
- account termination.

SoulMatch may act immediately where needed to protect users and platform integrity.
