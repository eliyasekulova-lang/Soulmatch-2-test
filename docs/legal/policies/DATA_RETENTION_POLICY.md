# SoulMatch Data Retention Policy
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026

This Data Retention Policy explains how long SoulMatch keeps personal data, when data is deleted or anonymized, and how user deletion rights are handled.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to personal information processed through SoulMatch web/mobile services, including account, profile, matching, messaging, safety, and operational records.

## 2. Retention by Account Status
### 2.1 Active Accounts
For active accounts, SoulMatch retains data as needed to provide and improve services, operate safety controls, and comply with legal obligations.

Typical categories retained during active use:
- account and profile information,
- matching and interaction metadata,
- safety and abuse-prevention records,
- audit and security logs required for platform integrity.

### 2.2 Inactive Accounts
If an account is inactive, SoulMatch may retain data for a limited period to:
- allow account reactivation,
- maintain fraud/safety protections,
- meet legal and compliance obligations.

Inactive account records may be deleted or anonymized after defined retention windows unless continued retention is legally required.

### 2.3 Deleted Accounts
When a user requests deletion and identity is verified:
- account is marked for deletion workflow,
- data is removed from active systems according to deletion procedures,
- backup data is purged on scheduled backup-retention cycles.

## 3. Deletion Timelines
SoulMatch uses staged deletion timelines:
- **Active-system deletion:** initiated promptly after approved deletion request.
- **Operational propagation:** related systems are updated in a controlled processing window.
- **Backups:** deleted data is removed from backups on scheduled backup purge cycles, subject to technical constraints.

Where immediate physical deletion is not technically possible (for example, immutable backup snapshots), data is isolated from active use and removed at the next eligible purge cycle.

## 4. Anonymization Procedures
Where full deletion is not required or not legally possible, SoulMatch may anonymize/de-identify records by:
- removing direct identifiers (for example, email/phone),
- replacing account identifiers with irreversible pseudonymous tokens,
- retaining only aggregate or de-identified analytics for system measurement.

Anonymized datasets are maintained so individuals are not reasonably identifiable.

## 5. Legal Retention Requirements
SoulMatch may retain limited data beyond normal deletion timelines where required by law or legitimate legal process, including:
- fraud prevention and investigation,
- security incident response,
- dispute handling,
- compliance with court orders, regulatory obligations, or law-enforcement preservation requests.

When legal holds apply, retained data is restricted to required scope and duration.

## 6. User Deletion Rights
Subject to applicable law (including PIPEDA and other mandatory requirements), users may request:
- deletion of personal information,
- access to retained categories,
- correction of inaccurate records,
- withdrawal of consent for consent-based processing.

SoulMatch may require identity verification before fulfilling deletion requests.

## 7. Governance and Review
This policy is reviewed periodically and updated as legal, operational, or technical requirements change.
