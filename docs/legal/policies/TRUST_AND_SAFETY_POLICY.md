# SoulMatch Trust & Safety Policy
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026

SoulMatch uses internal safety systems to detect:
- spam,
- fraud,
- harassment,
- coercion,
- abusive behavior.

Possible safety actions include:
- message limits,
- verification requirements,
- visibility adjustments,
- feature restrictions,
- account suspension.

Safety actions are applied to protect community safety and platform integrity. SoulMatch does not disclose internal detection algorithms or risk scoring methods.

SoulMatch does not apply psychological or medical labeling in trust and safety enforcement.
