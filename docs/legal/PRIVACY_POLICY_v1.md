# Privacy Policy (v1.0)

Status: Draft for attorney review before public launch.
Effective date: 2026-02-14
Jurisdiction baseline: Canada (PIPEDA), GDPR-ready, CCPA-compatible.

## 1. Scope
This policy explains how SoulMatch collects, uses, stores, shares, and deletes personal data for account, matching, messaging, moderation, and billing services.

## 2. Data Categories
- Account: email, authentication records, role, status.
- Profile: name and onboarding fields.
- Sensitive profile data: birth date/time/place and derived astrology vectors.
- User content: messages, media metadata.
- Safety/compliance: reports, blocks, appeals, moderation actions.
- Technical/diagnostic: IP, user agent, app version, logs, audit records.

## 3. Legal Basis
- Contract performance: account, matching, messaging, billing.
- Consent: sensitive birth-data processing and policy acceptance events.
- Legitimate interests: abuse prevention, fraud detection, service reliability.
- Legal obligations: law-enforcement and compliance response handling.

## 4. Sensitive Data
Sensitive birth data is processed only after explicit consent event capture. Withdrawal is supported and logged.

## 5. Retention
Retention is policy-controlled by data class (see retention rules table). Active-system deletion is immediate on approved erasure workflow; backup copies are purged on scheduled retention windows with audit evidence.

## 6. Data Sharing and Processors
Processors are tracked in the vendor register with purpose, data categories, transfer mechanism, and DPA reference.

## 7. International Transfers
Where cross-border transfers occur, safeguards such as SCC-equivalent mechanisms are applied.

## 8. User Rights
Users may request export, correction, deletion, restriction, and consent withdrawal. Requests are tracked via rights workflow and auditable status lifecycle.

## 9. Security
TLS at edge, encrypted storage, app-level encryption for sensitive profile fields, least-privilege access controls, and immutable audit logs.

## 10. Contact
Support: support@soulmatch.app

## 11. Legal Review Notice
This policy requires external legal counsel approval prior to public launch.
