# Law Enforcement Request Policy (v1.0)

Status: Draft for attorney review before public launch.
Effective date: 2026-02-14

## Intake Requirements
- Written legal request with jurisdiction and reference id.
- Validation by authorized legal/compliance owner.

## Processing Rules
- Requests are tracked in a dedicated law-enforcement register.
- Only legally valid, scoped requests are fulfilled.
- Every action is logged in immutable audit records.

## Emergency Requests
- Emergency handling is permitted only where law allows.
- Post-event legal validation and audit documentation are mandatory.

## Legal Review Notice
This policy requires external legal counsel approval prior to public launch.
