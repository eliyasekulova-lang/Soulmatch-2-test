How it works

03

Privacy & Trust

Your birth data is
yours. Always.

We collect sensitive information. We take that seriously. Here's exactly how we protect it.

🔐

Encrypted at rest

All birth data and PII is encrypted in the database. Not just hashed — encrypted with application-layer keys.

🛡️

TLS in transit

Every API call is made over HTTPS. No plaintext data ever leaves your device.

✅

Explicit consent

You consent to each data use case separately during onboarding. We record consent with policy version and timestamp.

🗑️

Deletion on request

Request account deletion and all your data is purged — including vectors, assessments, and messages.

🚫

No data selling

We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

⚖️

PIPEDA aligned

Canadian privacy law compliance with data retention policies, right-to-access, and audit trails.
