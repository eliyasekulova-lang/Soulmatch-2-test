# SoulMatch Acceptable Use Policy

Status: Draft for attorney review.

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all users of SoulMatch and supplements the Terms of Service.

## Prohibited Conduct
Users may not use SoulMatch to:

- harass, bully, threaten, stalk, intimidate, or exploit another person;
- coerce intimacy, manipulate vulnerable users, or pressure others for sex, money, housing, immigration help, or personal documents;
- impersonate another person or misstate identity, age, relationship status, or affiliation;
- commit fraud, catfishing, phishing, or financial scams;
- promote violence, hate, trafficking, sexual exploitation, or illegal conduct;
- publish or send non-consensual intimate content;
- involve minors in any romantic, sexual, exploitative, or otherwise prohibited interaction;
- send spam, chain messages, repetitive bulk content, or unauthorized advertising;
- scrape, copy, or data-mine the service;
- use bots, scripts, or automation to create accounts, message users, or bypass controls;
- probe, test, or interfere with the security, moderation, or ranking systems;
- upload malware or harmful code;
- evade blocks, suspensions, or safety restrictions.

## Enforcement
SoulMatch may investigate violations and take action that includes:

- content removal;
- hidden-message treatment;
- warnings;
- message limits;
- reduced visibility;
- account verification requirements;
- temporary suspension;
- permanent ban;
- preservation of evidence and audit logs;
- referral to law enforcement when legally required or appropriate.

SoulMatch may use automated signals and manual review to enforce this policy.
