# SoulMatch Consent Notice

Status: Draft for attorney review.

SoulMatch asks users to acknowledge and consent to processing that is necessary to run the service, including:

- creating and securing an account;
- storing profile details and matching preferences;
- processing birth date, birth time, and birth place for compatibility features;
- generating recommendations, rankings, and compatibility explanations;
- recording analytics and behavioral signals tied to product usage;
- detecting abuse, fraud, spam, coercion, and other unsafe behavior;
- maintaining audit trails, consent logs, and rights-request records.

Current onboarding flow requires:

- agreement to the privacy policy and service terms; and
- separate consent to processing sensitive birth data for astrology-enhanced matching or future activation of that matching mode.

Counsel should confirm whether this notice should be split into:

- general privacy acknowledgment;
- explicit sensitive-data consent;
- profiling or automated-decision disclosure;
- regional addenda for GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, and U.S. state privacy laws.
