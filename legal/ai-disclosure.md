# SoulMatch AI and Automated Processing Disclosure

Status: Draft for attorney review.

SoulMatch uses automated systems and model-driven logic in several parts of the product, including:

- compatibility scoring and match ranking;
- astrology-enhanced vector generation;
- psych and behavior profile generation;
- analytics and experimentation;
- trust-and-safety risk detection and escalation.

## What These Systems Do
These systems help personalize recommendations, reorder candidates, analyze interaction patterns, detect potential abuse or suspicious conduct, and support moderation workflows.

## What These Systems Do Not Do
SoulMatch does not describe its outputs as medical, clinical, psychological, psychiatric, diagnostic, or therapeutic advice. Match outputs are suggestions only, not guarantees or factual statements that any relationship will succeed.

## Human Oversight
Safety and enforcement actions may rely on automated signals plus human review. SoulMatch reserves the right not to disclose internal model features, thresholds, or anti-abuse logic where disclosure would undermine safety or security.

## Attorney Review Questions
Counsel should confirm:

- whether additional automated-decision notices are required;
- whether the profiling language is adequate for EU/UK and certain U.S. state regimes;
- whether users need a more explicit right to object, appeal, or request human review for some categories of decisions.
