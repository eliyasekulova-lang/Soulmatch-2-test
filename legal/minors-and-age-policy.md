# SoulMatch Minors and Age-Gating Policy

Status: Draft for attorney review.
Effective date: [insert date]

## Minimum Age
SoulMatch is intended only for adults. The current product configuration blocks sign-up if a user is below the minimum age threshold, which is presently set to 18 years.

## Age Data
Users provide date of birth during onboarding. That information is used to determine basic age eligibility and to power compatibility features.

## Child Safety Escalation
If reports or moderation reason codes indicate possible child-safety issues, including `minor`, `csam`, or `sexual_content_minor`, SoulMatch’s compliance flow is designed to:

1. route the case to a critical review queue;
2. preserve evidence metadata and audit entries;
3. restrict exposure while the review is pending;
4. document escalation and enforcement actions.

## Enforcement
Violation of child-safety rules may result in immediate restriction, suspension, termination, evidence preservation, and legal escalation where required.

## Counsel Review Required
Counsel should confirm:

- whether the age gate is sufficient for launch jurisdictions;
- whether additional age-assurance language is needed;
- when and how to notify authorities or third parties;
- what records must be preserved and for how long.
