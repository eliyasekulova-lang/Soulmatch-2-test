✦ The process

Four minutes to your psychological profile

No endless questionnaires. An adaptive assessment that gets smarter as you answer — and actually tells you something true about yourself.

STEP 01
🧠

Take the adaptive assessment

A short, adaptive question set maps you across 12 psychological dimensions — attachment style, emotional regulation, values, conflict approach, and more. Takes about 4 minutes.

STEP 02
🔍

Get your psychological profile

See a clear, honest breakdown of how you show up in relationships — strengths, patterns, and the dimensions that matter most for long-term compatibility.

STEP 03
🎯

Romance or Friendship — or both. Each mode uses different compatibility weights because what makes a great partner is not the same as what makes a great friend.
